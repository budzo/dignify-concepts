A LITTLE CLARITY

Before you begin.

What is a Dignify Snapshot?

A personal reference for your dignity traits, motivations, and communication preferences. Use it to understand yourself and share how you prefer to be treated.

Can I get started for free?

Yes. Dignify’s free starting experience reveals your Top Trait and first Dignify Country. Additional Snapshot features may require an upgrade.

Is this just for work?

No. Use Dignify with friends, family, and colleagues to build understanding in the relationships that matter to you.
