OUR STORY

It started with
a different conversation.

Dignify’s roots reach back to a Green Bay advertising business founded in 2002. A board game with Joe Kiedinger’s stepson, Alex, inspired a new approach: understanding someone starts with learning their communication preferences.

That personal insight grew into Dignify.
